Before you use this

Slope colors are calculated from coarse open elevation data and can be several degrees off. The avalanche terrain layer is a model. The heatmap only shows where people happened to record activities. None of it knows anything about today's snowpack, weather, or what has already slid.

Use it to find ideas worth investigating, then read the forecast at varsom.no, bring the gear and the training to use it, and make your own decisions in the terrain. This is not an avalanche safety tool, comes with no guarantee of any kind, and is used entirely at your own risk.
